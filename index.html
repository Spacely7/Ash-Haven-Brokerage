Who We Are

A brokerage built around clear, professional guidance

Ash Haven Insurance Brokerage Services Ltd. works alongside individuals, families and businesses to help them understand their options and find insurance solutions suited to their circumstances. As brokers, our role is to represent your interests, explain your choices in plain language, and stay involved beyond the point of sign-up.

From a first consultation to claims support, our team is a single point of contact — reducing the back-and-forth that insurance can often involve.

  • Client-facing, independent brokerage guidance
  • Support for both personal and business insurance needs
  • Assistance carried through to the claims process
