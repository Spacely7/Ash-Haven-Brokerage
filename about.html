About Ash Haven

A brokerage focused on clear guidance and steady support

Get to know Ash Haven Insurance Brokerage Services Ltd. — our mission, vision, core values, and the approach we bring to every client relationship.

Company Introduction

Who We Are

Ash Haven Insurance Brokerage Services Ltd. is an insurance brokerage based in East Legon, Accra. We work with individuals, families and businesses to help them understand the insurance options available to them and choose solutions suited to their needs.

As brokers, we act on behalf of our clients — explaining terms in plain language, comparing available options, and staying involved through the life of a policy, including when it's time to make a claim.

Our Mission

To provide individuals and businesses with clear, professional brokerage guidance that makes finding suitable insurance cover more straightforward.

Our Vision

To be a dependable insurance brokerage partner for clients across Ghana, known for professional guidance and responsive, client-focused service.

Our Approach

How We Work With Clients

Every client relationship begins with listening. We take the time to understand individual or business circumstances before discussing available options, so that guidance is relevant rather than generic.

Our support does not end once a decision is made. We remain a point of contact for questions, adjustments, renewals, and claims — aiming to make insurance feel less like paperwork and more like an ongoing relationship.
